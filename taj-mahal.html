THE STORY BEHIND THE MONUMENT

A Monument Built on Love

The Taj Mahal is a magnificent white marble mausoleum located on the banks of the Yamuna River in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. It was commissioned by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Construction began in 1632 and the main mausoleum was completed around 1648. The surrounding complex was completed in subsequent years. Today, the Taj Mahal is admired worldwide for its beauty, symmetry and historical significance.

DESIGN & ARCHITECTURE

Beauty in Every Detail

The Taj Mahal is celebrated for its symmetrical design, central dome, four minarets and intricate marble decorations. Its architecture combines elements of Persian, Islamic and Indian design traditions. Delicate floral patterns, calligraphy and inlay work add to the monument's artistic beauty. The changing colours of the marble throughout the day make it one of the world's most admired architectural creations.
