A SACRED LANDMARK

Sri Venkateswara Temple

Sri Venkateswara Temple is located in Tirumala, near Tirupati in Andhra Pradesh. Dedicated to Lord Venkateswara, a form of Lord Vishnu, it is one of the most visited Hindu pilgrimage centres in the world.

Situated among the beautiful Tirumala Hills, the temple is admired for its traditional Dravidian architecture, magnificent gopurams, gold-plated vimana and centuries-old traditions.

THE STORY BEHIND THE TEMPLE

History of Tirupati Temple

A Heritage of Devotion

The Sri Venkateswara Temple has a long history associated with Hindu traditions and devotion to Lord Venkateswara. It has been an important centre of pilgrimage for centuries.

Several South Indian dynasties, including the Pallavas, Cholas and Vijayanagara rulers, contributed to the temple's development and supported its religious and cultural activities.

Today, the temple is administered by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD), which manages its religious activities and visitor services.

DESIGN AND CRAFTSMANSHIP

Temple Architecture

The Sri Venkateswara Temple is a remarkable example of Dravidian temple architecture. Its design includes towering gopurams, beautifully carved pillars, sacred halls and detailed stone sculptures.

The gold-plated Ananda Nilayam Vimana above the main sanctum is one of the temple's most distinctive architectural features. The temple complex also includes sacred water bodies and traditional ceremonial spaces.
