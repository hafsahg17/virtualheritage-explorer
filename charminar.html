THE STORY BEHIND THE MONUMENT

The Icon of Hyderabad

Charminar is one of the most recognised historical monuments in India. It is located in the heart of Hyderabad, Telangana. The monument was constructed in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, the fifth ruler of the Qutb Shahi dynasty. It is widely associated with the founding and development of Hyderabad. The name Charminar comes from two words: "Char" meaning four and "Minar" meaning towers. The monument stands as an important symbol of Hyderabad's history, culture and architectural heritage.

DESIGN & ARCHITECTURE

Four Towers, One Timeless Legacy

Charminar is known for its four grand minarets, each rising from the four corners of the structure. Its architectural design combines Indo-Islamic elements with Persian influences. The monument features large arches, decorative stucco work, balconies and a mosque on its upper floor. Its symmetrical layout makes it an important example of the architectural achievements of the Qutb Shahi era. The monument continues to attract visitors, photographers and history enthusiasts from across the world.
