OUR PURPOSE

Preserving the Past, Inspiring the Future

Virtual Heritage Explorer is a digital heritage platform designed to make India's cultural treasures more accessible. Through historical information, multimedia content, multilingual audio guidance and planned AR integration, the project aims to create an engaging way to learn about and appreciate our country's heritage.
